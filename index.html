Our Story

From factory floor
to your doorstep.

For over fifteen years, Mahalaxmi Granites has been the quiet backbone of North India's granite trade. We travel to the quarry towns of Rajasthan and the cutting yards of Hyderabad, hand-select the finest blocks, and bring them to a single yard our dealers know and trust.

No middlemen, no inflated rates. Just straight-from-factory slabs, consistent grading, and dispatch schedules that keep your projects moving.
